How it's calculated

Every tick the pig first moves by its motion, then motion Y becomes (Y − 0.08) × 0.98 and X/Z are multiplied by 0.91. The height is the sum of every upward step, which works out to roughly 50 × motion − 3.92 × ticks of flight.

Check: one explosion (motion 44.65) gives 1,744 blocks, matching the measured ~1,750.

Assumes motion adds up with no loss while charging. Per-explosion motion Y is 44.6514 − (−0.0784) = 44.7298.
